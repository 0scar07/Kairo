FAQ

What everyone asks first.

Anything else? Open an issue on GitHub.

Is it free?

Yes. Kairo is free with no ads. It's also open source under the MIT license: you can see how it works on GitHub.

Do I need an account?

No. Look up any player by their Riot ID, #TAG or name and that's it. For notifications, each phone registers with its own token: we never ask for an email or password.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes, with the web version. Open it in Safari, tap Share and then "Add to Home Screen": it works like any other app, with no App Store. Some animations may differ, and game notifications are Android only for now.

Which games does it support?

Nine: League of Legends (with live game), Teamfight Tactics, Brawl Stars, Clash Royale, Clash of Clans, Dota 2, Fortnite, Apex Legends and PUBG. Valorant will come when Riot enables its match API for Kairo.

Which languages is it in?

Español, English, Português, Français and Deutsch. It follows your phone's language automatically and you can change it in Settings.
